Meet buff, the notebook that remembers for you

By Ashwin Thillai · November 2026

You're in back-to-back meetings. Someone promises to send you a file. You agree to follow up on something. A colleague mentions a deadline. You tell yourself you'll remember. By Friday, you don't, and you walk into the next meeting with the same person having forgotten what you agreed.

I built buff for that. It's a notebook for iPhone and iPad, made for short work notes about people and projects. It tells you what's waiting, and it gets you up to speed before you meet someone.

Write it the way you'd say it

There are no folders to pick and no forms to fill in. Type "Lunch with Maya about Orion, send her the beta slides by Friday" and press Send. buff saves the note under today. It also notices that you promised to do something, so it adds a todo due Friday and files it under Maya and the Orion project.

Before you send, a small line shows what buff understood, such as todo · Fri · Maya Chen. After you send, it says what it saved, with an undo in case it got something wrong. "Bob will send the spec tomorrow" becomes something you're waiting on. "I need coffee" stays a plain note.

See what's waiting

buff opens on Today. It shows what's overdue, what's due today, and what other people owe you. Below that are the people and projects you've written about lately. There's a search box at the top and a place to jot a quick note without leaving the screen.

Tap All todos for the full list, sorted into today, this week, later and waiting. Tick a todo to close it, or swipe to push it to tomorrow. A widget keeps today's list on your Home Screen, and an optional morning notification tells you how many are due.

Get up to speed before you meet

Everyone you write about gets a page, and so does every project. The page opens with a short brief: how long since your last note, what they owe you, what you owe them, any open decisions, and your last few notes. Thirty seconds of reading, and you remember where you left off.

If you let buff read your calendar, Today also shows your next meeting with someone you've written about, with a button that opens their brief. buff can remind you ten minutes before. It only reads your calendar and never saves anything from it.

Change your mind

Touch and hold any note, from any day, to edit or delete it. Every change can be undone, because buff keeps the earlier text in a history file next to your notes.

Just ask

Notes, the screen where you write, also works like a calm terminal. You can type short commands like /week or /find budget, but you don't have to learn any. Start with @buff and ask in plain words, like "@buff what's waiting for me". buff answers, and shows the shortcut it used so you pick them up as you go.

Save from anywhere

You don't have to open buff to save a note. Share text or a link from any app, or tell Siri, "Add to buff". You can even add a PDF or Word file, and buff turns it into notes you can search.

buff has moods

buff sits just above the box where you type. It blinks while it waits. It smiles when you save a note. It raises an eyebrow when it doesn't understand you. It looks sleepy when there's nothing to show. Tap it, and it's ready for a question.

Your notes are yours

Your notes are plain text files in your own iCloud Drive. You can open them in the Files app or on your Mac. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. On iPhones and iPads with Apple Intelligence, buff can suggest a name to tag or a todo it noticed, using Apple's on-device model. It never changes anything until you tap. Without AI, everything above still works.

Try it

buff is free for iPhone and iPad. When you first open it, a short tour shows you around. The buff docs cover every feature.
