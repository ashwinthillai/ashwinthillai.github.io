Hi, I’m Ashwin.

My day job is doubting models until they earn my trust. Mostly catastrophe models, which estimate how much a hurricane or earthquake could cost. I lead the team that decides when a model is ready to use.

Lately I’ve been building small apps with AI, which means working with a model I can’t fully see inside. So I do what I know: I check. This blog is about what I build and how I find out whether it actually works.
